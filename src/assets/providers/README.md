# Provider Icons

Except for the OpenAI vector listed below, assets were retrieved from the
providers' official websites on 2026-10-03. These are
bundled locally so rendering does not require external network requests.
Raster icons larger than 64 px were resized to 64 px without changing the mark.
The white backgrounds of the Claude and Qwen favicons were made transparent
on 2026-10-09 so their marks blend into both themes.
The white outline on the GLM SVG tile was also removed on 2026-10-09.
Brand assets remain the property of their respective owners; downloading a
favicon does not imply an unrestricted license or provider endorsement.
